# F2 - Foundation Gate Consolidation and Human Review

> **Role:** Coordinator with independent verifier inputs
>
> **DAG node:** F2 / Foundation Complete
>
> **State:** Blocked until named evidence completed independent review.

## 1. Objective

Assemble human-reviewable Foundation Gate dossier. Determine exact readiness
level, supported capability matrix, evidence boundary, open risks, and only
successor DAG nodes genuinely unblocked.

## 2. Required inputs

- F0 component/data/evaluation records;
- accepted P-001 through required P-008 decisions;
- B1/V1a, B2/B3 if claiming data/certification readiness;
- L1/L3 and relevant V1b;
- TR1/TR2 and relevant V1b;
- risk updates and negative/repair records;
- current component/control-plane identities/checksums.

## 3. Required review questions

1. What exact level: Planning, Infrastructure, Real-data evaluation,
   Certification, or 100K ready?
2. What components/revisions/manifests are included?
3. What claims are rejected/non-certifying?
4. What capabilities have independent evidence?
5. What runtime/infrastructure is static/fake/untested?
6. What data is candidate/admitted/staged/frozen?
7. What is prior-active/recovery/rollback status?
8. What is temporal/policy/revocation boundary?
9. What risks remain open?
10. What exact packets unlock next?

## 4. Required artifacts

- Gate Dossier evidence index;
- capability matrix;
- consolidated input/component identity manifest;
- verifier summary and limitations;
- decision request;
- human decision record;
- next-DAG update.

## 5. Prohibited conclusion

Do not call Foundation Complete a SOTA, benchmark pass, 100K pass, 1M
readiness, or consumer production adoption unless separate named gates are
also accepted. Missing evidence lowers readiness level; it is never omitted.
