Honest bottlenecks

  • Drain dominates the wall: remaining drain under reader traffic took 4,654.4 s (49.0% of the 9,494.9 s wall); initial drain took 999.4 s. Drain throughput is the scaling risk toward larger corpora.
  • Repair is real work: repair_to_zero took 1,162.1 s (12.2% of wall) across 6 bounded iterations to bring residual debt from 5,847 to 0.
  • One measured run: a single run on one shared VM (CPUs 0–1, nice 19, numpy 2.5.3). No SOTA claims; larger scales are roadmap, not measured.
