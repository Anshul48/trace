Negative Findings & Bottlenecks (Research Transparency)

  • Superlinear Drain Scaling: Drain cost per document grows at approximately O(N1.5) (~1.49 s/doc in remaining phase); drain accounts for >85% of total wall time.
  • Repair Overhead: repair_to_zero consumed 2,234 s (~17.2% of wall) even with repair disabled in default paths.
  • Reader Contention: Concurrent reader threads roughly halve drain throughput (~2× penalty) during background drain cycles.
  • Cosine Cache Null Result: An in-memory cosine cache yielded no throughput gain under concurrency; constant-factor software caches do not bend the drain curve.
