What a skeptic asks

The hard questions, answered plainly

Does it find the right source?

Not as often as keyword search. On our 50 test searches, ordinary keyword search put the right source in its top ten for 82% of them; TRACE and exact search by meaning each reached 52%. Our 0.9985 measures how much of exact vector search's answer TRACE kept, not answer quality.

Does a matching fingerprint prove nothing relevant was skipped?

No. A matching fingerprint shows the raw text agrees with the fingerprint published alongside it. This checks consistency, not an independently authenticated historical seal or the metadata. The comparison measures agreement with exact search's selected passages, not whether relevant information was missed: 0.9985 of exact search's top ten kept.

Is 0.79% the whole cost?

No. It counts passage-scoring work only. Choosing the groups, building them and the end-to-end time are separate costs (recorded search-stage times: TRACE 1.2 s, exact scan 88 ms, keyword search 6 ms; the setups differ, embedding is separate, and we make no speed claim).

Is the saving from self-organization, or from ordinary grouping?

We have not separated the two inside TRACE's own engine. The groups come from an ordinary sorting step, and we measured how ordered they are, not how they formed. In a simplified stand-in (not TRACE's own router; the cost of choosing the groups is not counted), scoring about 0.80% of one part of the library found 98.78% of a passage's ten true closest matches through our groups, 92.45% through blocks of passages in arrival order, and 33.27% through random groups of the same sizes (receipts/order-measure/out-4-routing-ablation.txt). So the grouping matters, and part of the help is the order the passages arrive in. The order we measured shows up at every size we tested (10,000 to 200,000 passages, a flat band with no trend), and we do not claim it improves with scale. The control, with its caveats.

Does it work on public data?

TRACE has not been run there. A stand-in of ours gave dataset-specific results: FiQA about 95% at 7% scored, SciFact about 95% at 24%, NFCorpus 90% at 28% and 97% at 55%.
