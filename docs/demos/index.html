For the maintainer

Swap list: replacing a film

Every film on the site, including the one on the home page, comes from one file: docs/demos/films.json. To replace a film, copy the new 720p mp4, poster (jpg) and captions (vtt) into docs/demos/, change the names in that file, and push. To publish the 3-minute film, set youtube to the video id (the part after v=) in the entry whose role is main. To fill a tour or replay slot, give its entry a file (or youtube) and remove its fallback_href. Nothing else needs editing, and no page needs rebuilding.
